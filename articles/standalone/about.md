# About

I'm Flee, and I've been racing OoTR since 2020. I play in most main season tournaments, consistently play in the upper divisions in League, and also play in side tournaments here and there as well.

I decided to create this site because I noticed a lack of resources for both getting into competitive OoTR, as well as helping players improve. While these do exist, they can sometimes be scattered on youtube, google docs, or discord discussions that are lost to time. My hope is that consolidating this information in one place can make it easier to learn the game and to have a place to point people to.

## Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about the site, or if you're interested in contributing, feel free to reach out to flee_ on discord.
