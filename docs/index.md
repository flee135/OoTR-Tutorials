# Introduction

Welcome to my collection of Ocarina of Time Randomizer tutorials! Whether you're just starting your randomizer journey, or you're a veteran looking to improve at the game, you've come to the right place.

Unless stated otherwise, all articles are written with a focus on the [standard ruleset](https://wiki.ootrandomizer.com/index.php?title=Standard) using glitchless logic.

## Categories

### Beginner
You've either never played OoTR before, or have only done a few seeds casually. The goal is to be able to complete seeds on your own, even if it takes multiple sittings. In these tutorials you'll find recommendations for seed settings, as well as tools and other resources to help you get started.

### Intermediate
You have multiple seeds under your belt and are looking to get faster. The goal is to feel comfortable participating in pickup races and tournaments. You'll learn essential tricks that can significantly speed up seeds.

### Advanced
You've done some races and want to put up more competitive times. The goal is to improve your chances of qualifying for tournaments or moving up in league divisions.

### Regions
This section breaks up the game into its different regions and lists tricks and tips in each one. These can be browsed regardless of your skill level. In any region, the tips will be roughly ordered that they should be prioritized.
