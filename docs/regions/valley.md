# Gerudo Valley

## Important

- (Adult) You can peek the hammer chest by charging a spin attack. While on top of Epona, you are also high up enough to see the chest if it is big.

- (Adult) With chus, you can peek the crate item. You can longshot the crate from the ledge on the other side of the valley. You can also do a hover into a recoil jumpslash to reach the ledge.

- (Adult) When grabbing the items at the bottom of valley, it is common to deathwarp to get back to the top, either from octorok damage, bomb damage, or drowning. If you're planning on doing this, you should enter the carpenter tent first to set your spawn point there.

## Good to Know

- (Adult) From the crate, you can jumpslash recoil to get to the bottom ledge in order to get the hint and waterfall freestanding.

## Optimizations

- When doing a deathwarp with bombs, you can pull ocarina when you get hit, and then repeatedly put away and pull out ocarina to get hit multiple times with the same bomb.
