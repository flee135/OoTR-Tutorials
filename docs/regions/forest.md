## Important

- Upon entering, side hop right x3, angled dry roll release target, then aim your hookshot straight up to climb the vines. This is a consistent way to aim the hook without spawning the wolfos. If you have longshot, then as soon as you enter you can longshot directly to the top.

- In the east courtyard, you can hookshot up to the vines. This trick is often in logic in tournament settings.

- In the push block room, you can use a [jumpslash recoil](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4OagS1QfvOs) to get to the floormaster. Angle is fairly lenient and you can aim it with C-up. Sometimes, hovers are logically required to get a key there, and this trick helps bypass that.

- You can set FW in the twisted corridor, and when you return, its twisted state will be whatever it was when you set FW. For example, it's common to set FW in the twisted version, then untwist it to go get the BK chest and floormaster, and then FW back. The corridor will be twisted again and you can just continue without having to shoot the eye switch again.

- The real purple poe will do a spin after all four poes spawn. She also spawns one frame earlier than the other poes, so if you pay enough attention you can figure out which one is real immediately.

- The real phantom ganon is both brighter and makes a sound compared to the fake one.

## Optimizations

- In the rotating platform room, it is possible to go fast enough and jump onto the second platform and immediately get a shot off to melt the ice.

- In the falling ceiling room, you can backflip onto the chest if it's small. Then, when the ceiling falls, you can backflip onto the fallen ceiling and continue to the door instead of waiting for the ceiling to rise again. The timing to get onto the chest is tight, and if the ceiling rises too far after you've jumped onto it, you will void.
