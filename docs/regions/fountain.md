# Zora's Fountain

## Important

- (Adult) You can peek the freestanding at the bottom of fountain with C-up while you're swimming, or by diving to get a closer look.

## Good to Know

- (Adult) You can climb onto the icebergs that tip over by equipping hovers when climbing, or by mashing jumpslash during the climb animation.

- (Adult) If you don't have explosives, you can still get to the fairy fountain with str2 and hammer by lifting the silver boulder and hammering the boulder underneath. You can then jump into the hole and and climb up on the other side.
