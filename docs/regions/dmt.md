# Death Mountain Trail

- To get the freestanding item above DC, you can stand at the ledge above and backflip at an angle over the short fence where the bomb flower is as child. If you are child without strength, you have to backflip next to the bomb flower so you don't try to grab it, but close enough to the fence to clear it.

- As either age, you can backwalk past the falling volcano rocks without getting hurt.

- You can peek the wall chest. As adult, you just need walk against the slanted wall and c up. As child you need to do a normal roll peek.

- When coming from Goron City, you can roll peek through the right wall to see the freestanding item.

- When turning in the eyedrops to Biggoron, if you have bolero, you can avoid walking all the way from lake to trail. If you play bolero *without* Goron tunic equipped, the heat timer takes precendence over the expiring eyedrops timer. When walking out to trail, the timer reverts back to the eyedrops, and you need to turn it in quickly. You can do this by mashing rolls up-left, and during the cutscene of Biggoron getting up, mashing eyedrops.
