## Good to Know

- (Adult) It's possible to catch big poes with just a bow and without Epona's. If you stand next to the sign outside market, poes will continuously spawn, with a chance of being a big poe. If a small poe spawns, you can kill it and wait until the next spawn.

- (Child) If you are heading to market but you won't make it to the drawbridge before nighttime, you can use a trick called [Schrödinger's Chain](https://oot-speedruns.fandom.com/wiki/Schr%C3%B6dinger%27s_Chain) to enter at night.
