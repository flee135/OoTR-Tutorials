# Ganon's Castle

## Important

- The first chest in shadow trial can be reached by spawning song of time blocks.

- You can cross shadow trial in several ways.
    - With longshot, you can longshot to the torch, then stand on the very edge to longshot to the like-like. You can then hover to the next platform then to the switch.
    - With fire arrows, you can light the torch to spawn platforms.
    - If you have longshot and dins, but not hovers, you can longshot the torch to light it with dins. You have enough time to cross the platforms and make it to where the chest spawns. It is possible to then jump to the switch from the invisible path.

- In spirit trial, you are expected to hit the switch with a bombchu, but you can also use bow extension to shoot the switch through the collision.

## Good to Know

- For the two iron knuckles, with BGS you can get behind and spam crouch stabs hitting both to kill them before they can attack. With master sword, you can get two crouch stabs to damage them, then roll through them to dodge the attack, then do the remaining crouch stabs.

- In shadow trial, if you spawn the chest and it's big, but you only have a hookshot, it's possible to get to the very edge and use hook extension to reach the chest. Alternatively, you can void and redo the room.

- (Trials on) For water trial, you can skip most of the push block room. Just push the first block to the left against the wall, then jump from the block to the ledge. You can then hammer jumpslash through the red ice to hit the switch.

- (Trials on) For fire trial, wearing hovers makes it so that the main falling platform doesn't fall. The smaller square platforms will still fall though.

- (Trials on) For spirit trial, you can burn the ceiling web by shooting a normal arrow through the lit torch from the previous room.
