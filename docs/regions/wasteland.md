# Haunted Wasteland

- It is worth learning how to cross wasteland without lens, both forwards and backwards.

- You can cross the sand from both directions with no items. Backwards, you can target the middle box and backwalk. Forwards, the most consistent way is 1 ESS turn left when entering and then backwalking. If you hate ESS turns, you can line yourself up at the left edge of the sand and backwalk parallel to the edge. Be wary of walking too far and voiding. You can hold target and shield as you're backwalking, and then release target but keep holding shield to immediately stop moving once you cross.
