# Haunted Wasteland

## Important

- You can cross wasteland without lens, both forwards and backwards. A good way to learn this is to using the collision viewer in the practice rom.

- You can cross the sand from both directions with no items. Backwards, you can target the middle box and backwalk. Forwards, the most consistent way is 1 ESS turn left when entering and then backwalking. If you don't want to ESS, you can line yourself up at the left edge of the sand and backwalk parallel to the edge. Be wary of walking too far and voiding. You can hold target and shield as you're backwalking, and then release target but keep holding shield to immediately stop moving once you cross.

## Good to Know

- As either age, you can jump to the carpet salesman. A jumpslash can help extend your jump to help make it, but it is not necessary.
