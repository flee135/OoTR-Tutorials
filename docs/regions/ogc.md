# Outside Ganon's Castle

## Important

- Entering OGC always sets time of day to the start of nighttime. This is useful, for example, if you're about to go child, you have Nocturne, and you want to do Dampe digging. You can enter OGC to get the perfect time of day, then change age, then warp with Nocturne.

## Good to Know

- You can collect the skull token without a hookshot by standing at the edge and backflipping.
