# Desert Colossus

## Important

- After Requiem, you can sidehop right, neutral roll, retarget to get the proper angle to backwalk to spirit.

## Good to Know

- When entering Colossus from Wasteland, you can turn around, sidehop right three times, and then backwalk the whole way to get into spirit.

- If you want to target to face a direction, usually to start a backwalk, but you keep targeting leevers, you can hit C up twice and then target. You cannot target anything right after leaving first person.
