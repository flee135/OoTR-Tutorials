# Desert Colossus

- When entering Colossus, you can target behind, sidehop right three times, and then backwalk the whole way to get into spirit.

- After Requiem, you can sidehop right, neutral roll, retarget to get the proper angle to backwalk to spirit.

- If you want to backwalk but you keep targeting leevers, you can hit C up twice and then start the backwalk. You cannot target anything right after leaving first person.

- Leevers cannot reach you if you are touching any wall.
