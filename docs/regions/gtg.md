## Important

- It's a good idea to start by going into the maze and peeking the chests to see how many keys you'll need. If you have song of time and a key already, you can open a door on your left to also peek the first chest in the climb, as well as the chest above the eye statues. This also gives you an easier time peeking the rest of the checks. The checks that you can see on the ground floor require 2 keys, 4 keys, 5 keys, and 7 keys respectively. Logically, you add 2 to each of those since the game assumes that in the worst case you also used two keys on the right side.

- As you're clearing GTG, it's a good idea to count how many keys you've seen. Keeping a tally on the side is a good way to do this and not forget. Often, toilet will be your last check, and since it's slow, it's good to be able to deduce if your 9th and final key is there so that you can skip the check.

- Learning to do hookless GTG is very useful since only toilet hard requires the hookshot. This requires learning the [silver rupees and flame wall skip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pS3Rq6s0WIo). For the following room, you need to ground jump to the alcove above the fake door, or backflip onto the chest and hover to the alcove (works regardless of the chest size).

## Good to Know

- If you don't have song of time or all 9 keys, you can save scum two keys on the right side of maze to see what the checks are, and if you find nothing useful, you can reset to get your keys back to then use on the left side.
