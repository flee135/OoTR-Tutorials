# Ice Cavern

## Important

- It's often a good idea to equip two bottles to reduce the amount of back and forth you need to do between the blue fire sources, and also to have an extra blue fire after completing the dungeon to bring back to KZ.

- After finishing Ice, if your next play is to leave and unfreeze KZ, you can go back where you came from and jump into the void in the ice puzzle room. This puts you back at the start of the dungeon.

## Good to Know

- You can skip the ice push puzzle with [glitchless jumps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SBVAb83hgAg). If you have bombs, though, you can ground jump to each alcove.
