# Kokiri Forest

## Important

- (Child) From the Kokiri sword chest, you can play sun's song to warp back out to the last entrance you came from. This is much faster than walking back out of the boulder maze.

## Good to Know

- (Adult) From the bottom, you can hook the fence to get to the entrance of Lost Woods. If you don't have hook, you can jump and grab the roof of Mido's house and then jump to the top instead of climbing the vines.

- (Adult) With hovers, you can hover off the root of the twins' house and grab the roof in order to get to the skulltula.
