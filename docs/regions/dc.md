>**Note:** The tricks here assume you are adult, since beating the dungeon as adult is substantially faster than as child. Anything that is for child will explicitly say so.

## Important

- If you don't need any checks in DC, you can [use chus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g4KLU9UghWc) from the bottom to light the Dodongo eyes.

- In the bombable stairs room, you can reach the gold skulltula in the alcove with a longshot.

- When you re-enter the lobby on the second floor in the southwest corner, before crossing the bridge, you can [roll peek](images/dc_bridge_peek.png) the wall on your left, facing north. From here, you can see the chest at the end of the bridge.

- You can jump straight to the platform leading to the bomb chest, which lets you skip a majority of the rooms on the top floor. If you have bombs, you can also ground jump to the chest.

## Good to Know

- (Adult) If you are going through DC with chus and no bombs, you can kill KD with chus using ground jumps or backflips.

- (Adult) You can lower the stairs with a bow. This is useful if, for example, you are doing DC with chus and want to conserve chus. Shoot a bomb flower on the left, then as quickly as you can shoot the second or third bomb flower on the right side.

- (Child) You can reach the lobby switch with a ground jump.

- (Child) If doing the top floor of DC, you can skip the flame puzzles without needing a slingshot.

## Optimizations

- The fastest way to blow up the stairs is with [two bombs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miGYrim0Jho). Most people put the bomb on the ledge first, then the one in the corner.

- For lowering the stairs with just strength, you can target the left wall when you enter, spam sidehops until Link picks up a bomb flower, shield drop, pick up the bomb flower in front of you, then throw that bomb flower into the corner, similar to the two bomb strat.
