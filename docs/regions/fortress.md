# Gerudo's Fortress

## Important

- You can peek the top chest from below. The easiest spot is right after you climb the vines. If you don't see a chest, you have to trust that it's small.

- When leaving the carpenter jail cell, you can longshot to the vines instead of walking around.

- You can get to the top chest directly with a longshot. With only hookshot, you can summon a scarecrow at the top, which you can hit if you stand right at the edge.

## Good to Know

- After getting captured, you can peek the top chest as you're leaving. If you have longshot, you can reach the chest from the prison with good positioning and hook extension.

## Optimizations

- When leaving the carpenter jail cell, you can play Epona's immediately to spawn her on the path to HBA. Otherwise you risk spawning her at the bottom of fortress.

- After HBA, it is faster to backwalk to GTG than to take Epona. After HBA, getting to wasteland is roughly the same amount of time on foot or on horseback.
