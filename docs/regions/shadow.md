## Important

- For dead hand, you can stand just outside an arm's grab range, then jumpslash into into it. The hand will catch you midair and drop you immediately, which spawns dead hand but does not result in you getting stuck. For the rest of the fight:
    - With master sword: Crouch stab, then normal slash. While dead hand is walking away, jumpslash the second time it swings its left arm. If timed correctly, you'll get two hits.
    - With BGS: Crouch stab, then immediately charge a spin attack while following dead hand. When you have a greatspin charged, release it. Alternatively, you can time a jumpslash like you would with master sword.

- For the truth spinner, going counterclockwise, the odds of the skull being correct is 0, 4/9, 2/9, 3/9, 0. When doing the truth spinner, you can equip hover boots while pulling the block, then walk away anticipating the floor to fall from under you. Every time you reload the room, the real skull will change.

- In the falling crusher room, you can time a backflip right when a crusher is falling, which will clip you to the top. You need to do this near the edge of the crusher where it is thinnest; if you are close to the center, you will not clip.

- For the pot freestanding check, you can drop FW before entering the room, get the freestanding item from the pot, then return FW to skip killing all the keese to open the door.

- To blow up the pot, you can pull a chu when entering the room, walk up against the pot, and drop the chu on the fourth red flash.

- To get onto the boat, you can jump off the ledge and onto the wheel of the boat, or you can hookshot to the top of the ladder. You should only pull the block if you want to open the shortcut.

- After coming out of the back of shadow, you have a few options for how to lower the bridge.
    - When shooting the bomb flowers with arrows, you can stand by the edge, shoot the arrow, and when the arrow connects with a bomb, sidehop into the void. This skips the cutscene of the bridge falling. Do NOT do this until after you come out from the back of shadow, otherwise you will respawn back before the boat ride.
    - If you have longshot and scarecrow song, you can stand on the edge of the debris and summon Pierre.
    - If you have chus, you can send a chu to the other side to blow up the bomb flowers.

## Good to Know

- For the two gibdos, you can turn left/right, sidehop to the center, then jumpslash. Then turn around and jumpslash the other gibdo.

- In the hallway with three skulltulas, you can roll three times, then turn up right and throw a nut. If spaced well, this can stun all three skulltulas.

- Instead of waiting for the platform that moves up and falls down, you can jump straight down to the right. You want to aim further left than you think.

- You can get through the fan room without irons or getting stuck by the fans using specific movement.
