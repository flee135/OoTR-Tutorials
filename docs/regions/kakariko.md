# Kakariko Village

## Important

- The man on the roof can be reached as adult by jumping to the roof of the potion shop. He can be reached as child by climbing the watchtower, targetting in the back left corner, and sidehopping left.

- (Adult) From the open grotto, you can break the left crate for 20 rupees (daytime only). To quickly get to the windmill, you can then jump from the crate onto the fence, and then from the fence to the ledge above, and then straight to the windmill front door.

## Good to Know

- (Adult) When leaving the the skulltula house, you can hook the box in front of the shooting gallery instead of walking around to the stairs. Likewise, from the shooting gallery, you can hook the building across to head to trail.

- (Child) To get the cucco next to the skulltula house, you can stand below and throw a nut to make it run off. Alternatively, you can stand diagonally on the other side of the fence, sidehop and jumpslash onto the other side.
