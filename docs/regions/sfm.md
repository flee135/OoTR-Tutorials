# Sacred Forest Meadows

## Important

- (Adult) There are multiple ways of skipping the maze.
    - You can play scarecrow song to hookshot to the top. You can even summon scarecrow from the initial position when you enter and longshot to the scarecrow.
    - With bombs, you can ground jump to the top.
    - With no items, you can curve a [jump](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5DG5KpGSsk) into the pool on the left to grab the ledge.

## Good to Know

- (Child) If you are leaving SFM after playing Minuet but fall into the maze, you can jumpslash the wolfos through the gate in order to open the gate.
