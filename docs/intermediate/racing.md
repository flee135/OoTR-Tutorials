# Racing

It's recommended that you're able to complete seeds in one sitting, but beyond that, there are no prerequisites to getting involved in races. Racing is a great way to practice the game, get involved with the community, and learning from other top players who played the same seed by studying their VODs or asking them questions about the seed and their decision making.

To get started, read up on the [racing](https://wiki.ootrandomizer.com/index.php?title=Racing) page in the OoTR Wiki. You'll also want to brush up on the [Standard](https://wiki.ootrandomizer.com/index.php?title=Racing) ruleset, and making sure you know what is and isn't allowed for the races. If you're not sure about any of the rules or need help with anything, `#standard-discussion` is a good channel in the Discord to ask any questions.

People will typically ask for pickup races in `#standard-race-planning`. If you want to receive notifications when people ping for races, you can give yourself the Standard role in `#role-assignment`. You can also ping @Standard yourself in `#standard-race-planning` to notify anyone who might be interested in racing. Just mention what settings you want to play. It's ok if you don't know how to set up a race; someone will usually be able to help set it up.

---

If setting up to participate in races is too much of a hassle, or you're just feeling anxious about joining live races, it's perfectly fine to play them asynchronously. You can look for previous races that were run and play the same seed. There is an [async race tool](https://async-race.rawz06.fr/) that is great for this. You can look up previous races that happened and get the seeds without spoiling yourself on the results; just make sure you're picking races that have the settings you want to race. You can even simulate the race so that, while playing, the site will notify you of when players finished as you reach those times.

At the end of the day, just have fun with it! Most people organizing and participating in pickup races are just looking to get some practice in, and are always happy to see new runners participating.
