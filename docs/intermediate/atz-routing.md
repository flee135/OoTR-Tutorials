# Atz's Routing Tutorials

Below each video are my annotations with timestamps for the concepts in the video and contextualize it for modern rando. I recommend watching the video while also following along with my tutorial at the same time.

## Routing Tutorial 1

**3:30** - The ranking of songs isn't too important; everyone nowadays knows how strong songs are and will route as many of them in early on. What's important is knowing why every song is as strong as it is, and how finding any given song might change what you are doing in the seed.

**9:22** - Gossip stones no longer require stone of agony to read.

You can choose to skip the section on openers if you want. It's covered better in the second routing tutorial.

**12:42** - These openings always involved vanilla spawns and child start, so keep that in mind for this section. There are no strict openers that you can reliably follow anymore, so instead think about what broad areas you want to clear early on.

**13:51** - Going to the ZL check to get a song was also required, and this needed to be done before you can get the songs from Malon or Saria, so routing in the ZL check very early was a must. Today, that check is given to you for free at the start of the seed.

**15:42** - atz mentions clearing Deku because seeds then almost always had open Deku, making almost all of it sphere 0. Settings now are usually Closed Deku and require Kokiri Sword. Heading to Kokiri Forest is still strong for the density and the song, though.

**17:23** - You can't always follow this opening strictly, so just prioritize getting the following done at some point early in the seed: Child Lon Lon, Child Kokiri Forest / Lost Woods / SFM, Adult Kak. This gets you to four songs, and most of child sphere 0 completed.

**19:06** - A lot of song checks might have opened up even in the early game, and I recommend getting those in the early game as well if they are super convenient, e.g. if you do adult Kak before child and you find warp songs that lead to other songs, you should get those songs before going child.

**22:42** - I don't agree with the idea of going somewhere because the "logic expects you to", like with atz's example of finding Epona's, hook, and bow early indicating that maybe the seed "wants" you to go to GTG. atz cautions against this, and I want to highlight that it's not a good idea and is purely speculative. Logic can be worth thinking about, but I wouldn't focus on it until the advanced tutorials.

**25:03** - Similarly to the last point, I also don't subscribe to the idea that things I found recently in the end game are more likely to lead to the the items I need. I typically still play for density in the late game when looking for my last item.

## Routing Tutorial 2

**3:20** - As mentioned in the tutorial 1 annotations, strict openers aren't really a thing anymore with random spawns and random starting age. The advice here still applies, and you should think of it as broad things you should aim to accomplish early in the seed. Again, these are Child Lon Lon, Child Kokiri Forest / Lost Woods / SFM, Adult Kak.

**9:00** - Since you are given the ZL check at the start, the pre-ZL opening isn't relevant anymore, and you can skip to 10:47 to see what your options are post-ZL.

**10:47** - From Lon Lon, if you don't have a good warp song or your savewarp is not in the forest area, I recommend walking out and going to valley (2 checks, 1 hint), lake (1 check, peek underwater, 3 hints, set up scarecrow song), field open grotto (1 check, 1 hint), into Kokiri Forest. If you have explosives you can get two more checks plus a hint in south field. This results in a more thorough child section, gives you a lot of hints to guide you through the mid game, and prevents potential backtracking in the late game.

As you improve, you can consider just going straight to Forest from Lon Lon instead. This defers your decision of if you want to clear valley/lake until you're closer to your mid-game.

**13:30** - I consider logic to be quite an advanced topic, and not one you need to prioritize early on while you're still getting familiar with the game.

That's not to say you should ignore it completely. When getting started, recognize what checks logically require what items. If you recognize that you've gotten something out of logic, just take a mental note of it. Make a habit of thinking "the game doesn't expect me to have X yet, and finding Y will put me back in logic." Over time, you'll get better at how to utilize that information.

**23:31** - Deku drops a bit in the priority list since it now typically requires Kokiri Sword. I put it roughly on par with Bottom of the Well.

**25:59** - atz mentions sometimes doing light trial before getting the light arrow hint, but I recommend just going straight for the hint, especially if you have Farore's. Even if the hint is Inside Ganon's Castle, it's still a 50% chance it's not in light trial.
