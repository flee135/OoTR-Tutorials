# Routing

Routing in randomizer is a loaded term. It can refer to what I'm going to call micro-routing, which is when you are in a region with a particular loadout, and need to follow a route to efficiently clear out the region. This has huge overlap with execution, because once you know these routes, you almost always do them the same way every single time. [Dungeon routing](./execution.md/#dungeons), covered in the execution tutorials, falls into this category.

Routing can also refer to macro-routing, which is making decisions on what to do next based on your loadout. Usually when people talk about routing, this is what they refer to, and is what will be covered here.

>**Note:** This section is dense with a lot of stuff to learn, and it's probably not a good idea to learn everything here before jumping into your next seed. I recommend learning a little bit at a time, mixing it in with reading [Execution](./execution.md) as well, and revisiting both pages between seeds to learn more or reinforce what you've already learned.

## Atz's Routing Videos

There are two very well known videos about routing from atz, who was considered the best player by far in the early days of rando. While these videos are now quite old and the seeds played back are very different from today's seeds, they are still very good videos that cover the fundamentals of routing.

If you'd like to watch these, you can visit this page on [atz's routing tutorials](./atz-routing.md), which has the videos embedded, alongside my own notes that you can follow with the video to help contextualize some of his tips for modern rando. If you don't feel like watching the videos, the main concepts are still covered below.
