# Routing

Routing in randomizer is a loaded term. It can refer to what I'm going to call micro-routing, which is when are in a location with a particular loadout, and need to follow a route to efficiently clear out the location. This has huge overlap with execution, because once you know these routes, you almost always do them the same way every single time.

Routing can also refer to macro-routing, which is making decisions on what to do next based on your loadout. Usually when people talk about routing, this is what they refer to.

We'll touch briefly on micro-routing, and then mostly go into macro-routing.

> **Note:** This section is dense with a lot of stuff to learn, and it's probably not a good idea to learn everything here before jumping into your next seed. I recommend learning a little bit at a time, mixing it in with reading [Execution](./execution.md) as well, and revisiting both pages between seeds to learn more or reinforce what you've already learned.
