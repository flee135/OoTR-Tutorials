# Next Steps

Congrats on completing your first seed! From here you have many different options if you want to keep playing.

## More Playthroughs

You can continue doing more seeds with the same settings. Each playthrough will be drastically different, so you don't have to change the settings if you want a fresh experience. If you do want to tweak some settings to help ease you into some more difficult settings, here are some things to try. You can do any combination of the following:

- Under "Shuffle Adult Trade Sequence, turn on everything from prescription onwards. This means you might have to do the latter half of the adult trade sequence for the Biggoron check, which can involve giving prescription to King Zora, eyeball frog to lab scientist, and eyedrops to Biggoron. Note that savequitting when holding any of these items will revert it back to a prescription.
- Adding back any checks that are disabled. One check you can keep disabled is Mask of Truth; this is disabled for nearly all tournament settings.
- Unchecking all the options in Minor Items in Big/Gold Chests. This means you will no longer find bombchus, shields, or nut/stick upgrades in big chests, and will have to start opening small chests if you are looking for any of these.
- Set Starting Age to Random
- Enable Randomize Overworld Spawns for child and/or adult. This changes where you spawn at the beginning of the seed, but also means that when you save and quit in the overworld, you will spawn in that random location. This can also have logical implication. For example, if your random child spawn is in Zora's Fountain, the game may expect you to beat the game without finding Ruto's Letter by savequitting in order to get to Jabu.

With all the of the above changes, you have a settings that resemble a traditional race seed. Most tournaments or pickup races have settings that are some variation on these settings.

When you have a couple of seeds under your belt, there are two routes you can go down.

## Getting Faster

Maybe you just want to get faster to be able to finish seeds in one sitting, or you want to look into doing some casual races with other runners. It's time to start diving into the [intermediate tutorials](../intermediate/intro.md).

## More Settings

If you want to explore what the randomizer has to offer, here are some of the most popular settings you can try.

- Shuffle Dungeon Entrances
- Tokensanity
- Shopsanity - 4 Items Per Shop
- Scrub Shuffle - On (Affordable)

There are so many other settings you can try besides these, and I encourage you to look through all the settings in the generator to see if anything else interests you.
