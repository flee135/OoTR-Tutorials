# Completing the Seed

Your goal in this seed is to defeat Ganon. In general, you'll need to:

- Figure out which dungeons are required.
- Find the items needed to complete those dungeons.
- Defeat Ganon.

## Early game

Feel free to explore whatever you want in the world. Eventually though, you'll want to make your way to the Temple of Time, where you can read the pedastal in front of the Door of Time to figure out what the dungeon rewards are. Reading the pedastal as adult will tell you where the medallions are, and reading the pedastal as child will tell you where the spiritual stones are.

In order to open the bridge to Ganon's Castle, you need the six medallions. Collecting the three stones only serves to play the cutscene in Hyrule Field as child to get another song, and may or may not be required.

I recommend taking notes on what the dungeon reward layout is and referencing it as you play the seed so you can always keep in mind which dungeons are actually required. Many trackers like HashFrog or Gossip Stone Tracker have spots to do this.

## Hints

You'll find gossip stones scattered throughout the world, and talking to one will give you a hint. These may be what a particular check may have, or what locations might contain important items. It's useful to take note of these as well whenever you find new hints.

## Mid-Late Game

I recommend making your way to locations that are dense with available checks to maximize your chance of finding items that you need. Check trackers can be useful for this to see at a glance where the densest locations are.

If you ever find that you can fully complete a required dungeon, getting those out of the way are a good idea too since you'll have to do it at some point to beat the seed.

## End Game

When you've found all six medallions, you can make your way to Ganon. To actually defeat Ganon, you'll need to be able to use light arrows, which requires having found the light arrows as well as a bow and magic.

If you have everything but light arrows, you can go straight to the Ganon fight, and he will tell you what region you can find them in to help narrow down where you need to look.

## Getting Stuck

If you're unsure how to progress, or your tracker says you're able to do something but you're not sure how, I'd recommend reading the [Glitchless Logic](https://wiki.ootrandomizer.com/index.php?title=Logic) wiki page. This breaks the game down into different regions and common pitfalls in each one. If you still can't find what you're looking for on this page, you can ask in the `#gameplay-and-tricks` channel in the discord.
